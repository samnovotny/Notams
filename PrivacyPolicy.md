# Privacy Policy

Effective 6 Oct 26

PocketNotams does not collect, store or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it contains no third-party code.

## Your location

If you sort or filter NOTAMs by distance, the app asks for permission to use your location while it is in use. Your location is used only on your device, to measure how far NOTAMs are from you and to show where you are on the map. It is never stored or sent anywhere by the app. The app stops using your location when you are not sorting or filtering by distance. You can turn location access off at any time in Settings on your device.

## Information stored on your device

The app keeps the following on your device only:

- the NOTAM briefings it downloads, so you can look back at earlier ones
- your settings and filters
- when you accepted the Terms of Use
- the latest message from the developer

None of this leaves your device. Deleting the app removes it all.

## Services the app connects to

To work, the app connects to:

- **NATS** (https://pibs.nats.co.uk), to download NOTAM briefings
- **samnovotny.github.io** (hosted by GitHub), to check once a day for a message from the developer
- **Apple Maps**, to draw maps

Like any website you visit, these services receive your device's IP address and basic request details when the app connects to them. The app sends them no other information about you. Their use of that information is covered by their own privacy policies.

## Feedback

If you choose to send feedback by email, I receive your email address and whatever you write. I use these only to reply to you and to improve the app, and I never share them.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
